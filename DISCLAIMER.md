## Simplified simulation

Real hardware varies. Sockets, connectors, header layouts and procedures differ between manufacturers and models. Hardware names in the game (for example "BENCH B75-ATX") are fictional. Always follow the documentation for your actual components.

## Safety on real hardware

When you work on a real computer:

- Disconnect mains power and switch off the PSU before opening the case.
- Use ESD protection (wrist strap, mat, ESD smock).
- Never force components into place.
- Follow your instructor's and the manufacturers' instructions.
