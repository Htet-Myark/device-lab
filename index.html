    🛠 PC Lab Assembly 3D

    A free 3D PC building simulator. Assemble a desktop PC on a virtual workbench, following a standard 7-part PC assembly lab procedure, and learn why each mistake matters before you touch real hardware.

    • Drag parts and cables from the parts tray onto the 3D bench. Valid drop spots light up as you hover.
    • Click / tap screws, levers, covers and switches when a step asks.
    • Click / tap a part in the tray to rotate or flip it. Pin 1, RAM notches and LED polarity matter.
    • The camera moves to each step automatically. Drag empty space to look around, and press V to snap back.
    Questions & step-by-step guide

    Is this PC building simulator free?

    Yes. It runs in your browser with no download, account or payment.

    Does it work on phones and tablets?

    Yes. Drag parts with your finger, pinch to zoom and drag to look around.

    Is it useful for CompTIA A+ or IT Essentials practice?

    It follows a typical PC assembly lab: ESD safety, CPU and RAM installation, standoffs, PCIe cards, SATA and power cables, and front-panel headers. It's good revision before a practical.
