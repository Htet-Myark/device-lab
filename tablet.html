    📲 Tablet Lab Repair 3D

    A free 3D tablet repair simulator. Assemble a tablet in 6 parts: the logic board and cameras, charging port and speakers, battery, LCD panel and front glass (digitizer). Then power it on and run the self-test.

    • Drag parts and cables from the parts tray onto the 3D bench. Valid drop spots light up as you hover.
    • Click / tap screws, the display and the power button when a step asks.
    • Click / tap the front glass to close it once everything is connected.
    • Tablet traps to watch for: LCD and digitizer connectors, the battery going in last, a long screw in a short hole, and the front-glass adhesive.
    Questions & step-by-step guide

    Is this tablet repair simulator free?

    Yes. It runs in your browser with no download, account or payment.

    What is the difference between the LCD and the digitizer?

    The LCD shows the picture. The digitizer is the touch-sensitive front glass. On many tablets they are separate parts with separate connectors, so a cracked glass can be replaced without a new LCD.

    Why connect the battery last?

    A connected battery can short-circuit components while you plug in other connectors. Disconnect it first when you open a tablet, and connect it last when you close one.
