    💻 Laptop Lab Assembly 3D

    A free 3D laptop assembly simulator for practising laptop repair. Assemble a laptop in 7 parts: the system board and ribbon cables, cooling, SO-DIMM memory, M.2 SSD and Wi-Fi, speakers and battery. Then close it up and power it on.

    • Drag parts and cables from the parts tray onto the 3D bench. Valid drop spots light up as you hover.
    • Click / tap screws, latches and modules when a step asks.
    • Click / tap a part in the tray to flip it. Ribbon contacts and SO-DIMM notches matter.
    • Laptop traps to watch for: ZIF latches, heat-sink screw order, M-key vs E-key slots, MAIN/AUX antennas, the battery going in last, and which USB-C port charges.
    Questions & step-by-step guide

    Is this laptop repair simulator free?

    Yes. It runs in your browser with no download, account or payment.

    Can I upgrade a laptop's CPU?

    Usually not: most laptop CPUs are soldered to the board. RAM (if it uses SO-DIMM slots), the M.2 SSD and the Wi-Fi card are the common upgrades, and this lab covers all three.

    Does it work on phones and tablets?

    Yes. Drag parts with your finger, pinch to zoom and drag to look around.
