    🛠 PC Lab Assembly 2D

    Assemble a desktop PC from a bare case, following a standard 7-part PC assembly lab procedure.

    • Drag parts and cables from the parts tray onto the bench.
    • Click / tap screws, levers, covers and switches when a step asks.
    • Click / tap a part in the tray to rotate or flip it. Pin 1, RAM notches and LED polarity matter.
    • Doing steps out of order or making a real-world mistake costs points.
